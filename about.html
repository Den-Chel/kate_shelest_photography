Hi, I'm Kate

I am a photographer based in London, Ontario. I specialize in women and maternity photography, but I also enjoy photographing families, portraits, weddings, lifestyle sessions and professional headshots.

My goal is to create elegant and timeless photographs that show each person's natural beauty and personality. I want every session to feel comfortable, relaxed and personal.

Whether we work in my studio or at another location, I will guide you through the session and help you feel confident in front of the camera. Together, we can create photographs that you will enjoy for years.
